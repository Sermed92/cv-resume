The story behind the name ↓
soyes yo.

Why soyesyo.com?

In Venezuelan slang, saying soy es yo is a playful way of saying “I’m a pro!”

When I ran World of Warcraft’s Mythic dungeons with friends, sometimes almost the whole group went down, leaving just the healer and me. As the tank, I kept the enemies busy while the healer kept me alive. If we managed to turn things around and finish the fight, out came soy es yo.

The same thing happens at work when I finally solve a problem I’ve been stuck on for several days. So when I needed a domain name, soyesyo.com felt like a natural choice.

If it made you smile, it was worth it.

I also build software.

I’m a software engineer specializing in backend development. I like making things work well, from a small tool to complex systems running in production.
