Why soyesyo.com?

In Venezuelan slang, saying soy es yo is a half-joking, half-serious way of saying “I’m the one who nailed it.”

It’s something I say when I’m gaming, solving a hard problem, or doing something I’m proud of. When I had to pick a domain, it was the most honest and fun name I could think of.

If it made you smile, it was worth it.

I also build software.
